Hey, I’m Dora.

I’m a product leader and founder with 10+ years of experience building AI products.

Until recently, I was the founder and CEO of Mohana Health, an award-winning femtech startup that helps women navigate hormonal health challenges by turning lab biomarkers into actionable insights about their bodies.

Before that, I was an AI research scientist developing LLMs for language to code translation (think Claude Code today). I had the privilege to do this work at one of the most reputable AI research institutions, Mila, working with Dima Bahdnau and Joelle Pineau.

Before Mila, I was at Shopify, as one of their first AI engineers where I led the foundation of personalization across 1M+ merchants’ stores (think app store, home feeds, product recommendations).

Outside of that, I really enjoy working with founders, and have advised 40+ startups on their AI and product strategy.

When I don’t do that, I enjoy long runs, kitchen experiments like fermentation and growing my own food, and teaching & practicing yoga.
